# Compliance guardrails

This repository is educational material about research method. The guardrails below keep it that way. A change that
breaks one of them is rejected, however useful it is.

## The basis

Under the SEC Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1 under the Investment Advisers Act), an "advertisement" is a communication
that offers an investment adviser's advisory services. In adopting the rule, the SEC said that educational material,
general market commentary and generic brand content generally do not meet that definition, and that communications
limited to general information about investing (types of investment vehicles, asset classes, strategies, regions or
sectors) do not offer advisory services. Whether a given piece qualifies depends on the facts and circumstances.

Sources:
- SEC adopting release, Investment Adviser Marketing, Release IA-5653 (2020): https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2020/ia-5653.pdf
- Federal Register version (March 5, 2021): https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2021/03/05/2020-28868/investment-adviser-marketing

This file is not legal advice. Anyone who uses this material in connection with an advisory business should have their
own compliance officer review how they use it.

## The guardrails

1. **No offer of services.** No firm name, logo, contact details, fee information or invitation to become a client.
2. **No specific securities.** No tickers or company names presented as examples of what to buy, hold or sell.
   Illustrations use made-up numbers.
3. **No performance.** No returns, track records, back-tests or hypothetical performance of any kind.
4. **No holdings or model contents.** Nothing about what any person, fund or model owns or how it is weighted.
5. **No testimonials or endorsements.**
6. **No paid-source text.** Ideas may be credited; text from subscription research is never copied.
7. **No trade timing.** Nothing that would let a reader anticipate when a specific portfolio will trade.
8. **Disclaimer stays.** The README's "educational material only" notice is never removed.

## If a firm adopts these rules

A firm may adopt or cite these rules internally. If it refers to this repository in client-facing material, that use
may itself be an advertisement and should go through the firm's own review first.
