# Contributing

## Proposing a change

Open a pull request that changes one rule or signal. Say:
1. What changes, in one sentence.
2. Why: the evidence or failure that prompted it, with public sources.
3. How it could fail, and how you would know.

Every change is checked against [COMPLIANCE.md](COMPLIANCE.md) first. A change that names securities, shows performance,
or offers services is closed.

## Review

A change is merged only after someone other than its author has read it. A new signal needs its formula, its inputs, when
it is checked, and its known blind spots ([signals.md](docs/signals.md) shows the shape).

## Versions

Releases are tagged (`v0.1`, `v0.2`, ...). A user that adopts these rules cites the version it follows. A change that
alters what a rule does is a new minor version; a wording fix is not.

## Style

American English. Plain, short sentences. Made-up numbers in every example.
